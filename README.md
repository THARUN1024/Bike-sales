## Overview
This dataset provides detailed information about customer behaviors and factors influencing bike purchases. It includes demographics, lifestyle attributes, and purchasing patterns of customers. The dataset can be used to analyze customer preferences, build predictive models, and help businesses improve their marketing and sales strategies.

---

## Skills Used
- **Data Analysis**: Analyzing customer behaviors and factors influencing bike purchases.
- **Predictive Modeling**: Building models to predict the likelihood of a customer buying a bike.
- **Customer Segmentation**: Grouping customers based on demographics and behaviors for targeted marketing.
- **Data Cleaning**: Handling missing values, duplicates, and inconsistent data.

---

## Key Insights

1. **Income Influence**:  
   Customers with higher annual incomes are more likely to purchase bikes. There is a clear trend where customers with higher income brackets tend to buy bikes more frequently.

2. **Education Level**:  
   Customers with a graduate degree or higher have a higher chance of purchasing a bike. This shows that education level is a key factor in the decision to buy.

3. **Marital Status**:  
   Married customers are more likely to purchase bikes compared to single customers, indicating that family-oriented individuals may prioritize bike purchases.

4. **Age Group**:  
   Younger customers (Adolescents and Middle Age) are more likely to purchase bikes, suggesting that age plays a role in purchasing decisions.

5. **Commute Distance**:  
   Customers who commute longer distances (10+ miles) are more likely to purchase bikes, possibly due to the convenience and practicality of bikes for long commutes.
